About and disclaimer

PharmaSim is an educational simulator with two modules. All patient cases are fictional and QC data are synthetic.

  • It does not diagnose, recommend individual treatment, or replace a pharmacist, prescriber or supervised training.
  • Clinical cases are marked requires faculty review and must be validated by a qualified pharmacy educator against current labeling before use in teaching.
  • A simulated QC pass is never evidence for releasing a real pharmaceutical batch.
  • Do not enter real patient information.
